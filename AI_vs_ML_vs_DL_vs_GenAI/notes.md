# Notes

## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is the field of making computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:
- Siri
- Google Maps
- Chess engines

---

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI.

Instead of writing rules, the computer learns patterns from data.

Example:
Netflix movie recommendations.

---

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.

It uses Neural Networks.

Used in:
- Face Recognition
- Speech Recognition
- LLMs

---

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI creates new content instead of only predicting or classifying.

Examples:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

---

## AI Hierarchy

AI
└── ML
    └── DL
        └── LLM
            └── Generative AI
